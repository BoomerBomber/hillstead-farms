Our Story

Five years to get here.

We bought this place in 2020 — fifty acres of Middle Tennessee hillside we couldn't live on yet. For five years it was the place we drove out to on weekends: walking the fence rows, mowing the fields, picking wild blackberries, and planning out loud on the tailgate.

In 2025 we finally moved home. And 2026 is the year it all actually started — the first real garden, the laying flock at full strength, our first sixty meat birds raised start-to-finish and stacked in the freezer.

We're not a generations-old farm, and we won't pretend to be one. We're a family learning fifty acres one season at a time — and filming it as we go, mistakes included.

— Andrew, Brittany & the kids
